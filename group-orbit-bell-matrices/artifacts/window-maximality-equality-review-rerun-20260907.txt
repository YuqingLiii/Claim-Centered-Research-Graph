## R2. Finite scope and implementation inspected

The root branch constructs the complete signed roots for each r=3,...,12;
checks the chosen two sign flips, common projected vector, rational intercept,
overlap n-4, equality at lambda_*, and improvement at four offsets above it.
It does not compute the optimum beyond lambda_* or establish the all-r theorem.

The PSD branch constructs eight Gram matrices from the exact input tuples
printed above. N is 1, 2, or 3. For each, lambda=d_min/4 is one strict-interior
parameter. It exhausts every independent ordered pair of 2N-dimensional signs
(16, 256, or 4096 pairs), retaining the entire maximizing-pair set. That set
is compared with all pairs (z,-z),(z,-z) from sign-quadratic maximizers of C.
These examples include singular PSD matrices, but are not a proof for arbitrary
C, arbitrary N, or every interior lambda. Universal verdicts are in the separate
window-maximality-equality-review-20260907.md analytic review.
