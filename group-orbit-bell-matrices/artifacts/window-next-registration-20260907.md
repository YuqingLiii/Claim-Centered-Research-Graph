Target: `GROTHORB.root-shift-window-maximality`. For the full D_r
projection and every r>=3, the known affine formula holds, among
lambda>=0, exactly on [0,1/(2(r-1))]. A proposed witness above the
endpoint has score equal to the affine expression plus
4lambda-2/(r-1). The coordinator's exploratory algebra suggested
removing one height-one positive root from the antipodal sign pattern
and choosing opposite symmetric signs on that coordinate. Registration
precedes author assignment, independent checking, and computational
testing, not the first exploratory idea. The witness and universal
quantifiers must be checked, not presumed correct.

## C18: strict-interior optimizer classification

Target: `GROTHORB.psd-antipodal-interior-equality`. For real PSD C
with strictly positive minimum diagonal, and 0<lambda<min C_ii/2,
all optimal ordered full sign pairs are exactly a=b=(z,-z), where
z maximizes z^T C z. The intended argument reconstructs the reviewed
nonnegative gap certificate and checks that strictly positive diagonal
coefficients force every x_i^2=1. This does not classify endpoint,
lambda=0, zero-diagonal, or out-of-window optimizers. Those are distinct
future scopes, not silently included here.
