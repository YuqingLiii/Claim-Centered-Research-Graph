Preregistered mechanism
- Cross-block handling: allocate disjoint sigma-pairs of Z_q to position blocks, omit the unique
  fixed point from every block, and average over equal-size pair allocations only after proving
  the fixed-allocation statement. Since each sigma-pair belongs to one block, a target-summing
  pair cannot cross blocks. This costs the alphabet budget: if a block receives r0 pairs, then
  m r0 <= (q-1)/2, so its phase-3 band is 2(1+floor(log_5 r0)), not automatically log q.
- Dual composition: derive the standard absolute-value composition directly. For an inner signed
  measure mu with ||mu||_1=1 and sum mu=0, sample from |mu| and expose the sign bit S=sgn(mu).
  For an outer OR_m dual Psi, use the density 2^m Psi(S_1,...,S_m) against the product |mu| law.
  Prove l1 normalization and falling-factorial degree multiplication from first principles.
- Required one-sided property: positive inner mass is supported only on block-YES inputs
  (equivalently, every block-NO input in the support has negative sign). For OR this makes S=+1
  a sound YES certificate. To retain constant composed correlation for growing m, the same
  mechanism is expected also to require inner conditional NO correctness
  Pr[g=NO | S=-1] = 1-O(1/m), not merely a fixed constant.

Predicted obstruction to test (not a conclusion)
- The phase-3 inner is NO-correct but has YES false-negative mass. If its correlation is rho, then
  the one-sided identities may force Pr[g=NO | S=-1]=rho. Under m independent blocks, the only
  uncertain outer sign pattern is S=(-1,...,-1), and its true all-NO probability becomes rho^m.
  Thus the standard composed correlation may equal -2 Psi(-1^m) rho^m <= rho^m. If rho is bounded
  away from 1 (the reviewed uniform guarantee is 437/581), this mechanism cannot keep correlation
  > 2/3 for growing m, and already cannot certify it uniformly for m=2 from that guarantee alone.
