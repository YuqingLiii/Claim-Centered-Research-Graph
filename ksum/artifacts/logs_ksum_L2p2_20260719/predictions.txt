KSUM.B4b.L2 phase 2 preregistration — 2026-07-19

Scope frozen before any phase-2 constructor or verifier is written or run.
Only the canonical D7-repaired route is in scope: odd q, all targets, degree target
c0*min{sqrt(N)*q^eta, N^(2/3)} for one fixed eta in (1/6,1/4], unit l1,
and correlation strictly greater than 2/3 with a fixed positive margin.  The
even-q all-cycle-type strengthening is out of scope.  No exponent claim will be
inferred from finite calculations (G11).

PROGRESS means: an explicit general-odd-q cap-corrected pair-lattice kernel family,
together with a proved annihilation lemma for a stated band reaching
c0*min{sqrt(N)*q^eta0,N^(2/3)} for an explicit eta0>1/6, a proved uniform
correlation bound at least 2/3+delta, proved l1 normalization, and exact-rational
verification of the claimed finite instances (N,q)=(5,5) and (7,3).

PARTIAL means: an explicit construction and exact-rational finite verification of
its claimed moment band, l1, and correlation, but at least one named step needed
for the general asymptotic statement remains unproved.  The report must identify
that step precisely and make no exponent claim from the finite checks.

BLOCKED means: the assault isolates a precise obstruction to simultaneously
annihilating the required moment class and retaining unit l1 and correlation
strictly above 2/3.  The obstruction must be stated as a candidate lemma-for-the-kill,
with its exact scope (for example a coefficientwise product-kernel subclass), and
must not be overclaimed as killing the full joint-histogram route without proof.

First candidate to test: coefficientwise total-occupancy conditioning of symmetric
per-pair signed kernels.  The key risk is that imposing zero constant and first
falling-factorial moments independently in every sigma-pair may force equal
absolute YES-interior and NO-axis mass locally; tensoring over r=(q-1)/2 pairs
would then make the global OR correlation decay exponentially after l1
normalization.  If proved, this is a BLOCKED outcome for the pure sign-separated
tensor ansatz and identifies the need for a genuinely correlated outer kernel.
