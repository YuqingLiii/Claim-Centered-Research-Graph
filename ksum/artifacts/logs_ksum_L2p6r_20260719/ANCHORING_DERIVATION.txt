Let H=S_(N-1) x C(sigma), V=R^X, and write the real H-module as

    V = direct_sum_lambda U_lambda tensor M_lambda,

where dim U_lambda=d_lambda and dim M_lambda=m_lambda.  H acts as
rho_lambda tensor I.  Therefore

    End_H(V) = direct_sum_lambda I_(U_lambda) tensor End(M_lambda),

and the symmetric PSD cone consists of blocks I tensor B_lambda with
B_lambda in Sym_+(m_lambda).  If Q_(lambda,j):M_lambda->V are orthonormal
frames for an orthonormal basis j=1,...,d_lambda of U_lambda, then

    A_lambda = sum_j Q_(lambda,j) B_lambda Q_(lambda,j)^T.

For every O_lambda in O(m_lambda), replacing

    Q_(lambda,j) by Q_(lambda,j) O_lambda,
    B_lambda by O_lambda^T B_lambda O_lambda

leaves A_lambda unchanged.  Hence a coordinate vector in M_lambda is not an
invariant of the irrep label lambda.  A Gelfand--Tsetlin chain for the symmetric
group resolves a basis of U_lambda (up to conventions); group-algebra/Jucys--Murphy
operators still act as the identity on M_lambda and do not resolve the copy space.
A canonical multiplicity basis can be added using extra ambient branching,
intertwiner, or joint-histogram harmonic data, but that is information beyond the
terminal irrep label.

For a finite group K, the Reynolds operator R_K=|K|^-1 sum_g rho(g) is the
orthogonal projector onto the trivial isotypic component.  It vanishes on every
nontrivial irreducible.  In the present use, if an H-satellite has no H-fixed
vector, then R_G v=R_G R_H v=0 because H is a subgroup of G.

Finally, if A is a nonzero PSD operator on R^X, diag(A) is entrywise nonnegative,
sum_x diag(A)_x=tr(A)>0, and a permutation Reynolds average preserves both the
sum and nonnegativity.  Thus mu=R_G(diag(A))/tr(A) has l1=1 and constant
factorial moment sum_x mu_x=1.  It cannot have positive annihilation band.
This conclusion applies to this diagonal/Reynolds transfer, not to centered,
off-diagonal, nonlinear, or otherwise signed extractions from optimizer data.
