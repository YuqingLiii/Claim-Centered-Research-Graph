# Boss question 2: are additivity counterexamples "many"?

Date: 2026-08-30

## Frozen question

Separate six meanings that must not be conflated:

1. existence;
2. probability in a named random ensemble;
3. relative openness at fixed dimensions;
4. positive intrinsic measure at fixed dimensions;
5. density, residuality, or full measure in the whole CPTP body; and
6. explicit printable witnesses and minimum dimensions.

## Current verdict

- Every strict fixed-dimensional witness persists in a diamond-norm neighborhood. Therefore, in
  those fixed dimensions, counterexamples contain a relative-open positive-intrinsic-measure set.
- In specified high-dimensional Haar models the probability is positive, tends to one, or is
  almost-sure eventually one, with the exact statement depending on the ensemble and limit order.
- The independently proved product-Haar theorem strengthens this to arbitrary prescribed finite
  MOE gap with probability tending to one after the target depth is fixed. Exact deterministic
  Weyl and flagged pushforwards give Holevo pair and same-channel gaps with at most one bit loss.
- Counterexamples are not dense, residual, or full-measure in the entire fixed-dimensional CPTP
  space: entanglement-breaking channels contain a nonempty open strongly additive region.
- The numbers 182/183 are thresholds for specific random-projection/Bell criteria, not global
  minimum dimensions.
- Algebraic witnesses and an eventually terminating exhaustive certified search exist in
  principle, but no practical small printable `p=1` matrix witness is currently supplied.

Thus "many" is true in local-open and named-random-model senses, and false as an unqualified
whole-space genericity claim.
