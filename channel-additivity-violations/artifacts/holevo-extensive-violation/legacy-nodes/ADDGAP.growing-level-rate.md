# ADDGAP.growing-level-rate — quantitative cost control for a growing partner

## Claim

For a hierarchical family of matched random-channel layers, prove a quantitative estimate strong
enough that

`sum_{j<=J}(g_j-2 ell_j) >= c sum_{j<=J} c_j-O(1)`

for some constant `c>0`, where `g_j` is the matched conjugate-pair minimum-output-entropy defect,
`ell_j` is the single-side fixed-partner leakage, and `c_j` is the full input/output/environment
qubit cost introduced by layer `j`.

Equivalently for the fixed-`k,t` BCN hierarchy, control the auxiliary thresholds `n_j` strongly
enough that the accumulated `Omega(J)` defect is also linear in the complete logarithmic channel
dimension, not merely in the number of layers.

## Role in DAG

This is the additional rate gate required for a Hayden--Penington-style extensive violation.
`ADDGAP.fixed-partner-global-entropy` alone would yield an absolute gap unbounded with depth; this
node is what turns that conclusion into an extensive one under the natural full-channel size.

## Current status

OPEN. Existing CFN strong convergence is formulated at every fixed matrix coefficient level. It
does not provide a convergence rate uniform when the partner dimension grows along the hierarchy.

## Dependencies

A quantitative growing-matrix-level strengthening of random-channel output-set/entropy convergence,
or an equivalent direct estimate of layer leakage versus added qubit cost, for the same family that
carries the matched conjugate defect.

## Strategy blueprint

Track the exact deterministic identity

`Delta(L_J,bar(L_J)) >= sum_j(g_j-2 ell_j)`

together with the exact logarithmic input, output and environment dimensions of every layer. The
proof target is a rate theorem that controls `ell_j` while the accumulated partner is itself
growing. Any proposed concentration or strong-convergence theorem must state its dependence on
the partner matrix level and must be strong enough to imply the displayed cost-weighted inequality.

Pointwise convergence for each fixed partner is not sufficient: it is compatible with thresholds
`n_j` growing super-exponentially, in which case an `Omega(J)` gap is sublinear in the full channel
size.
