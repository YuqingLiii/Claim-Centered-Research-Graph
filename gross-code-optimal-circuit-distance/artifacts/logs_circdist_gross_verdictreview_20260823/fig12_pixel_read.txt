*** AMBIGUITY FOUND AT PIXEL LEVEL (this is a defect of the SOURCE PAPER, not of phicert) ***
 Figure 10's caption states the convention: "Filled squares are X-type checks, empty squares are
 Z-type checks." In Figure 12 the UPPER panel's squares are FILLED (=> X-type by that convention)
 but its printed title says "Z-check CNOT schedule"; the LOWER panel's squares are EMPTY (=> Z-type)
 but its title says "X-check CNOT residuals". The titles and the fill convention CONTRADICT.
 The same contradiction is present in Figure 11 (page 20): upper "Z-check residuals" = FILLED
 squares, lower "X-check residuals" = EMPTY squares. So the conflict is systematic across both
 figures, not a one-off. The lower Fig-12 title also says "residuals" although the panel plainly
 shows CNOT times, which suggests the titles were copied from Figure 11.
 phicert assigns upper=X (following the FILL convention, against the printed titles) and states it
 swept "which panel is which check type (2)" as a free parameter. Whether the ambiguity is harmless
 therefore rests entirely on that sweep being real and the surviving object being invariant under
 the swap. That is checked separately in the review.
