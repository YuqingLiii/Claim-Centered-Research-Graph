## 3. Exact Phase-A finite synthesis

### 3.1 Exact-domain formula

For a named selector, XOR all data-qubit signatures into fixed signature `F`. At each participating check `c`, XOR its selected residual-kind position sets to `P_c`. The support choices are exactly the `C(6,|P_c|)` subsets of that row. For each choice `T_c`, let `s(T_c)` be the XOR of its qubit signatures. Split the checks into left/right halves and count full 84-bit XORs `A(v),B(v)`. With `lo(v)` the low 72 detector bits, the number of support tuples with zero detector syndrome and nonzero logical part is

\[
N_{\rm supp}=
\sum_{\ell}\Big(\sum_{v:\,lo(v)=\ell}A(v)\Big)
                 \Big(\sum_{w:\,lo(w)=\ell}B(w)\Big)
-\sum_v A(v)B(v).
\]

The subtraction removes full 84-bit zero, leaving a nonzero high logical word. The exact local-order count is

\[
N_{\rm order}=N_{\rm supp}\prod_c |P_c|!\,(6-|P_c|)!.
\]

Every option list, split count, support count, order count, and factorized proof hash was rebuilt. Constructive stored witnesses were checked as actual permutations whose full signature has low part zero and high part nonzero.
