# Term library, exact domains, and deduplication

Round 80 added 24 pre-outer whole-registry terms, 94 online terms, and 2107 post-candidate cross-registry terms. Final G=12,820.

Online retained validation covers 841,392 literal tuples and 55,037,740,032 factorized tuples. Cross-registry retained validation covers 19,978,824 literal pairs. Whole-registry validation adds 304,704 literal pair selectors and 35,294,400 factorized full tuples.

Every candidate is normalized to exact per-check 720-bit masks. Duplicate and dominance tests run against the full evolving library. A retained term has a nonempty exact product domain and explicit constructive selectors. Relation failures and deletion countertuples have only their explicitly tested scope.
