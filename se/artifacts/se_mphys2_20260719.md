## 1. Types and common trace identity

Use the R4a definitions

\[
 \mathcal C=P_\xi V_\lambda,\quad \dim\mathcal C=dc,\qquad
 \mathscr X_h=\bigoplus_{s\in\mathcal D_{a,b}}
 \operatorname{Ran}\Pi_{D_{h,s}},\quad
 \dim\mathscr X_h=N_{a,b}f^\theta,
\tag{1.1}
\]

\[
 L_h:\mathcal C\to\mathscr X_h,\qquad
 S_{h,\gamma}:\mathscr X_h\to\mathcal O,\qquad
 B_{h,\gamma}=S_{h,\gamma}L_h.
\tag{1.2}
\]

Thus \(L_h\) has \(dc\) carrier columns. Multiplicity \(c\) is in its
domain, not in \(\mathscr X_h\) or \(S_{h,\gamma}\). Put

\[
 A_\gamma=S_{h,\gamma}^*S_{h,\gamma}\succeq0,\qquad
 D_h=L_hL_h^*\succeq0,
\]

both acting on \(\mathscr X_h\). Then

\[
 \|B_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2
 =\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathscr X_h}(A_\gamma D_h).
\tag{1.3}
\]

The two Holder directions are

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(A_\gamma D_h)
 \le \|D_h\|\operatorname{Tr}A_\gamma
 =\|L_h\|^2\|S_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2,
\tag{1.4}
\]

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(A_\gamma D_h)
 \le \|A_\gamma\|\operatorname{Tr}D_h.
\tag{1.5}
\]

H1 is (1.4). Equation (1.5) is the reversed route and inherits the
coherent-fold operator failure when \(\operatorname{Tr}D_h\) is replaced
by \(T\).

## 2. H1: surviving direct trace pairing

### 2.1 Uniform-in-\(c\) input bound

For the canonical matching,

\[
 L_h^*L_h
 =P_\xi\left(\sum_{s\in\mathcal D_{a,b}}\Pi_{D_{h,s}}\right)P_\xi.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The physical matching sets are a subset of the complete colored orbit in
the blind-reviewed free-orbit identity. Every omitted summand is positive.
Therefore, on the full \(dc\)-dimensional carrier,

\[
 L_h^*L_h
 \preceq
 P_\xi\left(\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq A^\circ,\ |I|=a\\
 J\subseteq B,\ |J|=b}}\Pi_{I\sqcup J}\right)P_\xi
 =B_\theta(q)P_\xi.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Matching covariance transports (2.2) to every \(h\), so

\[
 \boxed{\|L_h\|^2\le B_\theta(q).}
\tag{2.3}
\]

This is not the unproved trace statement
\(\operatorname{Tr}(L_hL_h^*)\le T\), and it does not identify \(L_h\)
with the reviewed \(T\)-source map \(U_h\).

### 2.2 Exact frame trace and fold formula

Put

\[
 m=\binom ja,\qquad N=\binom{n-1}{j},\qquad N_{a,b}=mN.
\tag{2.4}
\]

For fixed \(L\subseteq A^\circ\), \(|L|=j\), define

\[
 \eta_\gamma(L)=
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left(
 P_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma\Pi_LQ_\gamma P_{\rm comp}\right).
\tag{2.5}
\]

The transport \(\rho(\tau_{h,s})\) is a unitary from the \(D_{h,s}\)
fiber to the \(L_s\) fiber and commutes with \(Q_\gamma\). After these
fiber identifications, all \(m\) colorings above a fixed \(L\) are
identical blocks. Consequently

\[
 S_{h,\gamma}S_{h,\gamma}^*
 \simeq m\sum_{\substack{L\subseteq A^\circ\\|L|=j}}
 P_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma\Pi_LQ_\gamma P_{\rm comp},
\tag{2.6}
\]

and \(S_{A^\circ}\)-symmetry gives

\[
 \boxed{\|S_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2=mN\eta_\gamma,}
 \qquad \eta_\gamma:=\eta_\gamma(L).
\tag{2.7}
\]

Since \(T=mNf^\alpha f^\beta\), equations (2.3) and (2.7) reduce H1 to
the following sufficient fiber conditions:

\[
 \boxed{
 \eta_0\le B_\theta(q)f^\alpha f^\beta,\qquad
 \sum_z\eta_z\le
 {B_\theta(q)f^\alpha f^\beta\over\delta_{\min}+1}.}
\tag{2.8}
\]

The stronger joint budget would follow from

\[
 \boxed{\eta_0+\sum_z(\delta_z+1)\eta_z
 \le B_\theta(q)f^\alpha f^\beta.}
\tag{2.9}
\]

Equations (2.8)--(2.9) are the surviving H1 frontier. They are independent
of \(c\), while (2.2) retains all multiplicity directions on the carrier.

### 2.3 Why the reviewed frame Grams do not prove (2.8)

The reviewed branch Grams give full-frame trace sums. If
\(r_\gamma=\operatorname{Tr}(P_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma)\), then

\[
 \sum_{\substack{D\subseteq(A\sqcup B)\setminus\{1\}\\|D|=j}}
 \eta_0(D)=B_\theta(q)r_0,\qquad
 \sum_D\eta_z(D)={B_\theta(q)\over\delta_z+1}r_z.
\tag{2.10}
\]

Equation (2.7), however, uses only the \(A^\circ\)-contained subfamily.
Positivity bounds it by (2.10), but does not recover the required
\(f^\alpha f^\beta\) count. For the long child,

\[
 r_0=f^{\tau_0/(n)}=\binom{n-1}{j}f^\theta=Nf^\theta.
\tag{2.11}
\]

The immediate reviewed-frame consequence can therefore lose
\(f^\theta/(f^\alpha f^\beta)\). This ratio need not be at most one:
\(\theta=(2,1),\alpha=(2),\beta=(1)\) has
\(c_{\alpha\beta}^{\theta}=1\), \(f^\theta=2\), and
\(f^\alpha f^\beta=1\). This does not refute (2.8); it shows why the
reviewed full-frame trace does not prove it. The blind-reviewed carrier
orbit identity cannot be inserted into (2.5), which has no \(P_\xi\)
compression.

## 4. H3: matching-averaged route

\[
 G_R={1\over n!}\sum_h

\tag{4.1}

per-\(h\) bounds are stronger than the assembly needs. Nevertheless,
reviewed right covariance gives

\[
 B_{h\cdot\pi,\gamma}=B_{h,\gamma}\rho_{\mathcal C}(\pi),
\tag{4.2}
\]

with a unitary right factor. Hence
\(\|B_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2\) is independent of \(h\), branch by branch.

## 5. H4: exact fold accounting

Equation (2.6) is the fold-counting identity. After grouping the domain by
uncolored \(L\), each fold is

\[
 F_m:(y_1,\ldots,y_m)\longmapsto\sum_{r=1}^m y_r,\qquad
 F_mF_m^*=mI.
\tag{5.1}
\]

If \(\widetilde S_{h,\gamma}\) is the unfolded, one-copy-per-\(L\) map,

\[
 S_{h,\gamma}S_{h,\gamma}^*
 =m\widetilde S_{h,\gamma}\widetilde S_{h,\gamma}^*,
\tag{5.2}
\]

so

\[
 \|S_{h,\gamma}\|^2=m\|\widetilde S_{h,\gamma}\|^2,\qquad
 \|S_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2
 =m\|\widetilde S_{h,\gamma}\|_{\rm HS}^2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

For \((a,b)=(1,1)\), \(m=2\). The unfolded/folded long norms are

\[
 {28\over25}\longmapsto {56\over25}\quad(\theta=(2)),\qquad
 {4\over3}\longmapsto {8\over3}\quad(\theta=(1,1)).
\tag{5.4}
\]

Relative to the proposed caps,

\[
 {28/25\over49/25}={4\over7}\longmapsto {8\over7},\qquad
 {4/3\over16/9}={3\over4}\longmapsto {3\over2}.
\tag{5.5}
\]

The tail and weighted spectra have the same factor two. H4 explains the
operator counterexamples and yields (2.7), but does not bound the unfolded
fiber scalar \(\eta_\gamma\). Its output is the reduced frontier
(2.8)--(2.9), not a completed proof.

\[

\tag{6.1}
\]

\[
 \|\mathsf G_{\rm can}^{\lambda,\xi}\|

\tag{6.2}
\]

\[

 \delta_{\min}\ge q-2j+1,\qquad
 {j\over q}\le\omega\le {j\over q-2j}.
