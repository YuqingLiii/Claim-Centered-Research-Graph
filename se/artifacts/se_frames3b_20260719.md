## 0. Result

The requested simultaneous factorization (F3.40)--(F3.41) does not exist
as stated.  Two of the mandatory $c=1$ gates violate a basis-independent
necessary consequence of those equations.

If

\[
 B_{h,\gamma}=K_{h,\gamma}U_h^*,\qquad U_h^*U_h\preceq I_T,
\tag{R3b.1}
\]

and

\[
 K_{h,0}^*K_{h,0}+
 \sum_z(\delta_z+1)K_{h,z}^*K_{h,z}
 \preceq B_\theta(q)^2I_T,
\tag{R3b.2}
\]

then necessarily

\[
 \boxed{
 B_{h,0}^*B_{h,0}+
 \sum_z(\delta_z+1)B_{h,z}^*B_{h,z}
 \preceq B_\theta(q)^2I_{dc}.}
\tag{R3b.3}
\]

Indeed, the left side of (R3b.3) is $U_h\mathcal K_hU_h^*$,
where $\mathcal K_h$ is the left side of (R3b.2).  Since
$U_h^*U_h\preceq I_T$, also $U_hU_h^*\preceq I_{dc}$.

The literal physical branches give the following exact violations.

| gate | $dc$ | $T$ | $B_\theta(q)^2$ | largest eigenvalue of the left side of (R3b.3) | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $(4,8,2),\theta=(2),(a,b)=(1,1)$ | 6 | 6 | $49/25$ | $833/300$ | $17/12$ |
| $(4,8,2),\theta=(1,1),(a,b)=(1,1)$ | 6 | 6 | $16/9$ | $32/9$ | $2$ |

Both values were obtained twice: first from the fixed-set formula
(F3.19), and independently from the literal (q!)-term BR Fourier kernel.
All comparisons use exact rational or symbolic matrices.

Thus this is stronger than a failure to find the right columns: no maps
$K_{h,\gamma}$ can satisfy (F3.40)--(F3.41) together with the reviewed
contraction (F3.38), even if the requirement that $K_{h,\gamma}$ come
from the R2 output frames is dropped.

The Hilbert--Schmidt conclusions (F3.42) still pass every requested exact
gate.  They do not follow from the refuted factorization.  The minimal

## 1. Spaces and physical indices

Retain the notation of (F3.3)--(F3.38):

\[
 \mathcal C=P_\xi V_\lambda,
 \quad \dim\mathcal C=dc,
 \quad \mathcal O=\operatorname{Ran}P_{\rm comp},
 \quad \dim\mathcal O=R.
\tag{R3b.4}
\]

For the canonical matching, put

\[
 \mathcal D_{a,b}=
 \{(I,J):I,J\subseteq\{2,\ldots,n\},\ |I|=a,\ |J|=b,
 \ I\cap J=\varnothing\}.
\tag{R3b.5}
\]

Its size and the reviewed raw-column count are

\[
 N_{a,b}=\binom{n-1}{a}\binom{n-1-a}{b},\qquad
 T=N_{a,b}f^\alpha f^\beta.
\tag{R3b.6}
\]

For $s=(I,J)\in\mathcal D_{a,b}$, write

\[
 D_s=I\cup\{\bar i:i\in J\},\qquad L_s=I\cup J,
\tag{R3b.7}
\]

and choose $\tau_s$ to swap each selected $\bar i$ with $i$.  The
sets are pair-disjoint, so these swaps commute, $\tau_s$ fixes the query,
and

\[
 \rho_\lambda(\tau_s)\Pi_{D_s}\rho_\lambda(\tau_s)^*
 =\Pi_{L_s}.
\tag{R3b.8}
\]

The uncompleted physical map is

\[
 \mathcal A_h=
 \sum_{s\in\mathcal D_{a,b}}
 \rho_\lambda(\tau_{h,s})\Pi_{D_{h,s}}P_\xi:
 \mathcal C\longrightarrow V_\lambda,
\tag{R3b.9}
\]

and the literal branches are

\[
 B_{h,\gamma}=P_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma\mathcal A_h:
 \mathcal C\longrightarrow\mathcal O.
\tag{R3b.10}
\]

Equation (R3b.10) is exactly (F3.19)--(F3.21), since every
$\rho_\lambda(\tau_{h,s})$ commutes with $Q_\gamma$.

**Type audit.**  In an ambient basis, $\mathcal A_h$ is $F\times dc$,
$B_{h,\gamma}$ is $R\times dc$, $U_h$ is $dc\times T$, and every
$K_{h,\gamma}$ is $R\times T$.  The Gram in (R3b.2) is $T\times T$,
while the physical necessary Gram in (R3b.3) is $dc\times dc$.  The
multiplicity (c) is included in (dc) and is never appended to a
$Q_\gamma V_\lambda$ branch.

## 2. S1: the explicit grouped-polar candidate

The direct per-\((I,J)\) polar decomposition is incorrectly typed when
$c>1$: $\Pi_{D_s}P_\xi$ can have rank $f^\alpha f^\beta c$, while the
raw index (s) supplies only $f^\alpha f^\beta$ columns.  Grouping all
physical colorings with the same output set is the smallest repair that
also handles duplicate output fibers.

For $L\subseteq\{2,\ldots,n\}$, $|L|=j$, define

\[
 C_{h,L}=
 \sum_{\substack{s\in\mathcal D_{a,b}\\L_s=L}}
 \rho_\lambda(\tau_{h,s})\Pi_{D_{h,s}}P_\xi:
 \mathcal C\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ran}\Pi_L.
\tag{R3b.11}
\]

Take its thin polar decomposition

\[
 C_{h,L}=V_{h,L}A_{h,L}^*,
\quad
 V_{h,L}:\mathbb C^{r_{h,L}}\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ran}\Pi_L,
\quad V_{h,L}^*V_{h,L}=I_{r_{h,L}},
\tag{R3b.12}
\]

where $A_{h,L}:\mathbb C^{r_{h,L}}\to\mathcal C$ and
$r_{h,L}=\operatorname{rank}C_{h,L}$.  Let

\[
 r_h^{\rm pol}=\sum_Lr_{h,L},\qquad
 V_h=\bigoplus_LV_{h,L}:\mathbb C^{r_h^{\rm pol}}\longrightarrow V_\lambda,
 \qquad
 A_h=\bigoplus_LA_{h,L}:\mathbb C^{r_h^{\rm pol}}\longrightarrow\mathcal C.
\tag{R3b.13}
\]

Then

\[
 \mathcal A_h=V_hA_h^*.
\tag{R3b.14}
\]

When $r_h^{\rm pol}\le T$, pad $V_h,A_h$ by zero columns to dimension
(T), and put

\[
 \sigma_h^2=\|A_h\|^2,
 \qquad \widetilde U_h={A_h\over\sigma_h},
 \qquad
 \widetilde K_{h,\gamma}=\sigma_hP_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma V_h.
\tag{R3b.15}
\]

These are explicit maps with the required dimensions, and

\[
 \widetilde U_h^*\widetilde U_h\preceq I_T,
 \qquad B_{h,\gamma}=\widetilde K_{h,\gamma}\widetilde U_h^*.
\tag{R3b.16}
\]

The map $P_{\rm comp}Q_\gamma V_h$ is an explicit subframe of the R2
fixed-set output frame: every column of $V_{h,L}$ lies in the physical
fiber $\operatorname{Ran}\Pi_L$.  Thus (R3b.15) is the requested
frame-side form of S1, rather than a Fourier row defined from the completed
branch $B_{h,\gamma}$.  The input map $\widetilde U_h$ has not been
identified with the reviewed abstract R1 map $U_h$; it is an explicit
diagnostic candidate built from the uncompleted fixed-set terms.

More explicitly, let

\[
 J_h^{\rm out}:\mathbb C^T\longrightarrow\mathscr F_j^{\neg1}
\tag{R3b.15a}
\]

place each column of $V_{h,L}$ in the (L)-fiber and send the padded
coordinates to zero.  It is an
$\binom{q-1}{j}f^\theta\times T$ partial isometry.  By the definition of
the R2 analysis frame,

\[
 \widetilde K_{h,\gamma}
 =\sigma_hP_{\rm comp}E_{{\rm out},\gamma}^*J_h^{\rm out}:
 \mathbb C^T\longrightarrow\mathcal O.
\tag{R3b.15b}
\]

Thus the frame-side factor is literally the reviewed R2 output-frame
adjoint followed by completion, with a displayed partial-isometry
selection.  The exact gates test whether its weighted Gram has the claimed
normalization; two gates show that it does not.

For a general matching, right covariance gives
$\widetilde U_h=\rho_{\mathcal C}(r_h)\widetilde U_0$, where $r_h$ is
the matching permutation from (F3.20); the frame-side matrices may be taken
from the correspondingly relabeled physical fibers.  All norms are
unchanged.

The candidate passes the identity and contraction checks in all nine
mandatory $c=1$ sectors.  It fails (F3.41) in exactly the two mixed-color
sectors listed in Section 0.  This failure cannot be repaired by another
polar scaling because (R3b.3) fails for the physical branches themselves.

For arbitrary (c), even the preliminary inequality
$r_h^{\rm pol}\le T$ is not known.  The per-sector $I_c$ lift would
replace (T) by (Tc) and is the already-refuted R2 typing.

## 5. S2 and the minimal typed successor

There is an exact output-side factorization that does not suppress
multiplicity.  Put

\[
 \mathscr X_h=
 \bigoplus_{s\in\mathcal D_{a,b}}\operatorname{Ran}\Pi_{D_{h,s}},
 \qquad
 \dim\mathscr X_h=N_{a,b}f^\theta.
\tag{R3b.21}
\]

Define

\[
 L_h:\mathcal C\longrightarrow\mathscr X_h,
 \qquad
 L_hx=(\Pi_{D_{h,s}}P_\xi x)_s,
\tag{R3b.22}
\]

and

\[
 S_{h,\gamma}:\mathscr X_h\longrightarrow\mathcal O,
 \qquad
 S_{h,\gamma}(y_s)_s=
 P_{\rm comp}\sum_s\rho_\lambda(\tau_{h,s})Q_\gamma y_s.
\tag{R3b.23}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{B_{h,\gamma}=S_{h,\gamma}L_h}
\tag{R3b.24}
\]

is an identity of literal matrices.  Here $L_h$ is
$(N_{a,b}f^\theta)\times dc$, $S_{h,\gamma}$ is
$R\times(N_{a,b}f^\theta)$, and (R3b.24) is $R\times dc$.

The R2 equality controls the complete output fixed-set frame, but it does
not separately control (R3b.23) after several physical colorings land in
the same output fiber.  On the input side, (R3b.19) shows that $L_h^*L_h$
is not the reviewed full colored scalar.  Consequently a product of an
ambient output operator norm and an input trace does not produce the
(T)-count.  This is the precise ambient-versus-carrier trace mismatch in
S2; moving $P_\xi$ last remains forbidden by (F3.43).

The minimal all-(c) successor is the following single joint estimate:

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal C}\!\left[
 L_h^*\left(
 S_{h,0}^*S_{h,0}+
 \sum_z(\delta_z+1)S_{h,z}^*S_{h,z}
 \right)L_h
 \right]
 \le B_\theta(q)^2T.}
\tag{M_{\rm phys}}
\]

The operator in parentheses is an endomorphism of $\mathscr X_h$; after
composition with $L_h$, the traced operator is $dc\times dc$.  The
right side contains the physical raw count (T) and no multiplicity
factor.  By (R3b.24), this lemma immediately gives both inequalities in
(F3.42): discard the positive tail terms for the long branch, and use
$\delta_z+1\ge\delta_{\min}+1$ for the tail sum.

\((M_{\rm phys})\) passes all nine exact gates, including the two rows that
refute (F3.40)--(F3.41).  It remains **CONJECTURED — pending review**.

A scalar countermodel shows why (F3.42) alone cannot recover the requested
factorization.  Take $B_\theta(q)=1$, $T=2$, one scalar branch
$B=\sqrt2:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$.  Then
$|B|_{\rm HS}^2=2=B_\theta(q)^2T$.  If
$B=KU^*$, $U^*U\preceq I_2$, and $K^*K\preceq I_2$, then
$|B|\le\|K\|\|U\|\le1$, a contradiction.

## 7. Rule-7 failure-mode audit

1. **Physical versus complete colored orbit.**  Equations (R3b.17)--(R3b.20)
   keep the two indexing sets distinct.  The reviewed Claims 3--4 are not
   applied to the smaller physical set.
2. **Output-fiber multiplicity.**  Different physical colorings can land in
   the same $\operatorname{Ran}\Pi_L$.  They are grouped before the polar
   decomposition; they are not declared orthogonal raw columns.
3. **Multiplicity typing.**  A per-\((I,J)\) polar factor can require
   $f^\alpha f^\beta c$ columns.  No tensor product with $I_c$ is used.
4. **Ambient versus carrier traces.**  The R2 equality is on $V_\lambda$,
   while the joint trace in \((M_{\rm phys})\) is on the \(dc\)-dimensional
   carrier.  No ambient trace is replaced by (T).
5. **Compression order.**  Only $P_{\rm comp}$ is moved through
   $Q_\gamma$.  The strict projector is never moved through $\Pi_D$, in
   accordance with (F3.43).
6. **Formula versus independent gates.**  The fixed-set construction and
   the literal (q!)-term kernel are separate code paths.  The latter does
   not use the candidate (K/U) matrices.
7. **Matching covariance.**  The no-go condition is preserved by the right
   unitary matching action, so a canonical-row counterexample refutes every
   relabeling of that sector.
8. **Typography.**  Every representation operator is written as
   $\rho_\lambda(\cdot)$, and every displayed map has an explicit domain
   and codomain.
