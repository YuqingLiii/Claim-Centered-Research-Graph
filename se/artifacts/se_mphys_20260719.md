There is a second, independent gap in A1(ii).  The reviewed source-column
contraction (R1 (6.6)) applies to
\(U_h:\mathscr R_{a,b}\to\mathcal C\), which has \(T\) source columns.
The map in the present statement is

matrix form.  These maps are not identified.  Although
\(\operatorname{Tr}(L_hL_h^*)\le T\) passes all nine \(c=1\) gates, the
raw-column argument does not prove it for all \(c\).

The desired conclusions themselves still pass all nine gates:

\[
\tag{T-a}
\operatorname{Tr}(L_h^*S_{h,0}^*S_{h,0}L_h)\le B_\theta(q)^2T,

\[

The stronger joint trace budget also passes all nine gates.  No all-\(c\)
proof follows.  Therefore (T-a), (T-b), \((M_{\rm phys})\), (F3.42),

\[

\tag{1.1}

\[
 S_{h,\gamma}:\mathscr X_h\to\mathcal O,\qquad

\tag{1.2}
\]

Hence \(L_h\) is \((N_{a,b}f^\theta)\times dc\),
\(S_{h,\gamma}\) is \(R\times(N_{a,b}f^\theta)\), and direct expansion
gives the literal \(R\times dc\) identity

\[
 B_{h,\gamma}=S_{h,\gamma}L_h.
\tag{1.3}
\]

For every positive endomorphism \(X\) of \(\mathscr X_h\), finite-dimensional
cyclicity gives

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal C}(L_h^*XL_h)

\tag{1.4}
\]

Consequently A1 would be valid if both

\[
 X_h:=S_{h,0}^*S_{h,0}
 +\sum_z(\delta_z+1)S_{h,z}^*S_{h,z}

\tag{1.5}

and

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(L_hL_h^*)\le T
\tag{1.6}
\]

held.  Equations (1.4)--(1.6) would imply \((M_{\rm phys})\), hence
(T-a)--(T-b) by positivity.  This derivation precedes the comparison with
R3b Section 5.  The exact gates below show that (1.5) is false and that the

For the separate A2 route one would instead need

## 2. Why the reviewed frame equality does not transport

The reviewed one-copy statement is

\[
 E_{{\rm out},0}^*E_{{\rm out},0}
 +\sum_z(\delta_z+1)E_{{\rm out},z}^*E_{{\rm out},z}
 =B_\theta(q)I_{V_\lambda}.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Its branch domains are the mutually orthogonal spaces \(Q_0V_\lambda\)
and \(Q_zV_\lambda\), and its analysis frame has one coordinate fiber for
each uncolored output set \(L\).

In contrast, \(\mathscr X_h\) has one coordinate fiber for each colored
pair \(s=(I,J)\).  The map

\[
 s=(I,J)\longmapsto L_s=I\cup J
\tag{2.2}
\]

has \(\binom ja\) preimages for each physical \(L\).  Equation (1.2) folds
those coordinates by addition after the transports
\(\rho_\lambda(\tau_{h,s})\).  For \((a,b)=(1,1)\), this is a two-to-one
fold.  It is not an isometric embedding of \(\mathscr X_h\) into the
one-copy frame space in (2.1).  The factor-of-two coherent directions are
visible in (0.1).

The branch projectors \(Q_z\) (and the corresponding projectors in (2.1))
are orthogonal, and \(P_{\rm comp}\) commutes with them.  This gives
orthogonal output ranges.  It does **not** imply that the positive operators
\(S_{h,z}^*S_{h,z}\) have orthogonal supports on their common domain
\(\mathscr X_h\).  Therefore a sum-to-maximum step for the \(S\)-Grams is
not licensed by branch orthogonality.  For the \(E_{\rm out,z}\)-Grams the
maximum is valid because their zero-extended domains are the orthogonal
\(Q_zV_\lambda\) spaces.  All nine present gates have one tail child, so
the tail sum is already a single term; the long-branch counterexamples do
not rely on this additional sum-versus-maximum hazard.

## 6. Multiplicity, range, and Rule-7 gates

domain of \(L_h\) is the full \(dc\)-dimensional carrier.  Therefore
\(L_hL_h^*\) contains all multiplicity directions through its range, and
its trace equals the carrier trace by (1.4).  A factor \(c\) cannot simply be
deleted: a no-\(c\) result needs either a genuine \(T\)-source contraction
identified with \(L_h\), or a direct trace estimate.  Neither is presently
available.
