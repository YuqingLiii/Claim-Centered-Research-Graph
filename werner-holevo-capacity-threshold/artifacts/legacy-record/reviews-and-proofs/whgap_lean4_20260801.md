## 1. What was proved (all in `WHGAP/Support/Channel.lean`, new §8)

The §6 witness is built as a genuinely **complex** object and then measured:

| declaration | statement |
|---|---|
| `psiWit r` | `ψ = (e₁ + i e₂)/r`, `r` a real variable carried under `r² = 2` (the `IcScalar` device for `β`: no `Real.sqrt` in the identity layer) |
| `Pwit_eq_outer`, `PwitBar_eq_outer` | `(ψψ†)_{jk} = ψ_j \overline{ψ_k}` **is** the explicit `Pwit` with entries `±1/2, ±i/2`, and likewise `P_ψ̄` |
| `rhoWit ε`, `rhoWit_eq`, `rhoWit_trace` | `ρ_ε = (1−ε)P_ψ + εP_ψ̄ = !![1/2, −i(1−2ε)/2, 0; i(1−2ε)/2, 1/2, 0; 0,0,0]`, trace 1 |
| `rhoWit_isHermitian` | Hermitian for real `ε` (`Matrix.IsHermitian`, i.e. `ᴴ`, not merely `ᵀ`) |
| `LamWit_eq` | `Λ_x(ρ_ε)` written out — the explicit `3×3` complex matrix, **in the standard basis** (where it is *not* diagonal; `IcScalar.BOut` is its diagonal form in the `(ψ, ψ̄, e₃)` frame) |
| `LamCWit_eq` | `Λ^c_x(ρ_ε)` written out — the explicit `4×4`, `β` free |
| `LamWit_isHermitian`, `LamCWit_isHermitian` | both outputs Hermitian for real `x, ε, β` |
| **`specB_charpoly`** | `charpoly (Lam x (rhoWit ε)) = (X − C((1−ε)(1−x/2)))·(X − C(ε(1−x/2)))·(X − C(x/2))`, for **all** `x, ε : ℂ` |
| **`specE_charpoly`** | `charpoly (LamC x β (rhoWit ε)) = (X² − C(1−x/2)·X + C(2x(1−x)ε(1−ε)))·(X − C(x(1−ε)/2))·(X − C(xε/2))`, under `hβ : β² = x(1−x)/2`, all `x, ε, β : ℂ` |
| `specB_charpoly_kraus`, `specE_charpoly_kraus` | the same two statements about the **Kraus-defined** objects `LamK s t` and `LamCK s t` (`s² = x/2`, `t² = 1−x`; `β = st` then satisfies `hβ` automatically) |
| `specB_charpoly_list` | the B-side charpoly **is** `((specB x ε).map (X − C ↑·)).prod` — the literal `IcScalar` B-list, cast to `ℂ` |
| `quad_eq_lamP_lamM`, `specE_charpoly_list` | the quadratic factor **is** `(X − C ↑lamP)(X − C ↑lamM)` and hence the E-side charpoly **is** `((specE x ε).map (X − C ↑·)).prod` — the literal `IcScalar` E-list — on `0 ≤ x ≤ 1` |

Plumbing (reusable, deliberately small): `split3 : Fin 3 ≃ Fin 2 ⊕ Fin 1`,
`split4 : Fin 4 ≃ Fin 2 ⊕ Fin 2` (the `{0,3} ⊔ {1,2}` split of the E-side output),
`charpoly_of_reindex_blocks`, `charpoly_fin_one_eq`, `charpoly_fin_two_eq` (split form from
trace/det), `charpoly_fin_two_quad` (quadratic form from trace/det).

**Why charpoly level, and why the E side is stated with the quadratic factor.** `λ±`
involve `√discE`; a root-level statement would drag `Real.sqrt` (and a domain restriction)
into the identity layer. At charpoly level the statement is square-root free and holds for
arbitrary complex parameters. `specE_charpoly_list`, which *does* name `lamP`/`lamM`, is the
one place where the square root reappears and it therefore carries `0 ≤ x ≤ 1`.

**Route note (no deviation from the frozen route).** The commission's route was followed as
written: the channel definitions of `Support/Channel.lean` instantiated at `ℂ` (see §0), the
two outputs written out entrywise, the charpolys computed. The only free design choice was
the *mechanism* for the two charpolys: both matrices are block diagonal after an explicit
reindexing (`split3`, `split4`), so `Matrix.charpoly_reindex` +
`Matrix.charpoly_fromBlocks_zero₁₂` + `Matrix.charpoly_fin_two` reduce everything to four
trace/determinant computations. No `det_fin_four` (which Mathlib does not have) and no
charmatrix expansion were needed. The two determinant steps are one `linear_combination`
each, over `Complex.I_sq` and `hβ`.

## 2. What is **not** proved — residual (b) is REDUCED, not removed

Recorded identically in `Trust.lean` (new dated subsection), in `Channel.lean`'s honesty
note item 4, and in the project README item 10:

> **"Same characteristic polynomial ⟹ same von Neumann entropy" is NOT available for
> complex Hermitian matrices in this development.**

`WHGAP/Support/Entropy.lean` defines `vnEntropy` — and proves `vnEntropy_congr_charpoly`,
`vnEntropy_eq_sum_of_charpoly` — for **real symmetric** matrices over `ℝ` only. The §6
outputs are complex Hermitian (proved so here), and the real layer does not apply to them
verbatim: `Matrix.IsHermitian.eigenvalues` and the `roots_charpoly_eq_eigenvalues` bridge
would have to be re-run over `ℂ`/`RCLike`. Consequently:

1. nothing here turns the two charpolys into `IcScalar x ε = S(Λ(ρ_ε)) − S(Λ^c(ρ_ε))`;
2. `hach` — the achievability *reading* of `IcScalar` — is untouched;
3. everything item 1/7/10 of the README already said remains true: no complete positivity,
   no Stinespring, no proof that `LamCK` is *the* complementary channel, no coding theorem,
   and `lsd_devetak` / `zw_hrsf_zero` / `q_pos_of_ic_pos` are still assumptions.

What *has* moved: row (b) of the `Trust.lean` residual table — "the spectra of `Λ_x(ρ_ε)`
and `Λ^c_x(ρ_ε)` are `specB`/`specE`" — is no longer an exact-sympy-only claim. It is a
Lean theorem about the Kraus-built channel, at characteristic-polynomial granularity, in
both the factored form and the literal `IcScalar`-list form.

Two further honest notes:

* `IcScalar.BOut`/`blockA`/`blockB` remain **real stand-ins**; WP-G does not prove they
  equal the complex outputs (they do not — they are different matrices in different
  frames). What is proved is that the complex outputs' charpolys split over exactly the
  two lists those stand-ins were built to carry, which is the content residual (b) named.
* `specB_charpoly`/`specE_charpoly` are stated for arbitrary complex `x, ε, β`. For complex
  parameters `ρ_ε` is not a state and the outputs are not Hermitian; the statements are
  polynomial identities, strictly stronger than the physical case, and the Hermiticity
  lemmas are separately stated for real parameters.
