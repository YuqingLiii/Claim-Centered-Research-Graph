the triple product at seven rationals (including `26/51`, `4/7`, `3/4`), conclude for all
`x` by Mathlib's vanishing-polynomial lemma.  Per the commission's explicit instruction

> (`artifacts/logs_whgap_lean2_20260731/wpa_pilot_timing.txt` + `.lean`)

The frozen route needs ≥ 14 such products (2 per point × 7 points) plus the traces: ≥ 90
min of kernel reduction inside a 120-min budget, and a permanent > 1.5 h rebuild cost for
the project.  **Not affordable.**

Rather than take the documented degradation path (7 sample points + `hdeg` as an explicit
hypothesis — a *weaker*, conditional result), the executor changed route to something
strictly stronger and far cheaper:

1. The fifteen forced coefficients are **constant combinations of the two eigenvalues**:
   `c_k(x) = a_k λ₅(x) + b_k λ₃(x)` with `a_k, b_k ∈ ℚ` (`12a_k`, `16b_k` integers).
   Hence `C_x = λ₅ Q₅ + λ₃ Q₃` with `Q₅ = R₅/12`, `Q₃ = R₃/16` **independent of `x`**.
   In Lean this is 729 entrywise goals that collapse to one `field_simp; ring` because the
   fifteen pattern values are shared atoms — cheap, and valid for all `x ≠ 0`.
2. `R₅` and `R₃` get explicit **integer rank factorizations** `R₅ = W₅V₅ᵀ`, `R₃ = V₃V₃ᵀ`
   (`V₅ : 27×5`, `W₅ = V₅·diag(3,3,1,3,3)`, `V₃ : 27×3`; all entries in `{0,±1,±2,±3}`),
   certified by four **small** decidable checks — `V₅ᵀW₅ = 12·1₅`, `V₃ᵀV₃ = 16·1₃`,
   `V₅ᵀV₃ = 0`, `V₃ᵀW₅ = 0` — plus two 729-entry factorization checks.  Idempotency,
   orthogonality, the traces (`Matrix.trace_mul_comm`) and symmetry follow by algebra.
   **No 27×27×27 product is ever reduced by the kernel.**

Consequences of the deviation, stated plainly: the result is **stronger** (all `x ≠ 0`, not

### Route deviation from the frozen commission — honest record

The commission's route guidance was "the paper's leading-term argument with explicit
bounds", with `λ₋ ∈ [detA/TrA, 2·detA/TrA]`-type brackets via `√(a²−b) ∈ [a − b/a, a]`, and
`negMulLog(εc) = εc log(1/(εc))`.  The commission explicitly said "improve freely;
mathematical rigor beats route fidelity".  What was implemented instead:

1. **Grouping instead of a global expansion.**  The seven `negMulLog` terms are split into
   three groups that are each handled exactly, with no error term at all in two of them:

       Ic = [nml((1-e)B) - nml(lam+)]           (G2)  >= 0
          + [nml(e B) - nml(c e) - nml(lam-)]   (G1)  >= d1 * nml(e) - 2e
          + [nml(c)  - nml(c(1-e))]             (G3)  >= 0

   with `β = 1 − x/2`, `c = x/2`, `μ_A = 2x(1−x)/β`.  G2 and G3 are proved **nonnegative**
   rather than bounded — the commissioned bracketing of the *remainder* is never needed.
2. **Two exact algebraic identities replace the `√(a²−b)` bracket.**
   `discE = (1−3x/2)² + 2x(1−x)(1−2ε)²` gives `discE ≥ 0` unconditionally, and
   `discE − β²(1−2ε)² = 4ε(1−ε)(1−3x/2)²` gives the **sharp** bracket `λ₋ ≤ εβ` (not a
   factor-2-lossy one).  Combining with `λ₊λ₋ = D` and `λ₊ ≥ (1−ε)β` yields the *sharp*
   `λ₋ ≤ μ_A ε`, which is what makes the surviving leading coefficient exactly
   `d₁(x) = (1−x) − μ_A(x) = (1−x)(2−5x)/(2−x)` (`dOne`, already in the module) with **no
   loss**.  The commissioned two-sided bracket would have given a lossy coefficient.
3. **Two elementary monotonicity lemmas replace concavity/derivative machinery.**
   `negMulLog_antitone` (on `[e⁻¹, ∞)`) and `negMulLog_mono` (on `(0, e⁻¹]`) are proved from
   `Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos` alone, in ~10 lines each — no `StrictConcaveOn`, no
   derivative, no interval integral.  The two `e⁻¹` side conditions are discharged from
   `Real.exp_one_lt_d9`/`gt_d9` as `1/3 < e⁻¹ < 2/5` (`exp_neg_one_gt`, `exp_neg_one_lt`),
   which is exactly why the witness window is capped at `ε ≤ 1/3`.
4. **The constant is `2`, from `Real.negMulLog_le_one_sub_self`.**  `nml β ≥ 0`, `nml c ≤ 1`,
   `nml μ_A ≤ 1`; hence the `O(ε)` bracket is `≥ −2ε`.  No `1/e` maximum, no `C(x)`.
