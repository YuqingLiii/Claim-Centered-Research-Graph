## 2. What is NOT proved (read this before citing anything above)

1. **`LamCK` is not shown to be *the* complementary channel.** This development has a Kraus
   sum (`LamK`) and a matrix of Kraus overlaps (`LamCK`); it has no complete positivity, no
   Stinespring dilation, no channel object, and no blocklength. The identification is a
   *definition-vs-referent* gap of the kind `Trust.lean` lists, not something a Lean
   hypothesis can carry.
2. **No capacity statement is reached.** `IcLB`, `lsd_devetak`, `q_pos_of_ic_pos`,
   `zw_hrsf_zero` remain interface fields with citations. Per §7.9: *a theorem proved against
   an abstract interface is exactly as strong as the results that interface cites, and not
   one notch stronger.*
3. **`hach'` is not logically weaker than `hach`.** They are equivalent on the applied range
   (`entropy_witness_eq_IcScalar`). What moved inside Lean is the *identification* of the
   number `IcScalar x ε` with the entropy difference of the two outputs. Anyone reading the
   commission's "reduced bridge" wording as a logical weakening would be wrong, and the
   sources say so.
4. **`vnEntropyC` is not "the von Neumann entropy of a state".** No positivity, no
   normalization, no density-matrix structure is assumed anywhere; `Real.negMulLog` is `0` at
   `0` and negative above `1`. `Channel.rhoWit_trace` checks trace one; PSD-ness of `ρ_ε` is
   nowhere represented in Lean.
5. **The real layer is not superseded.** `Support/Entropy.lean` is unchanged and still
   real-symmetric-only; the P14 payoff chain still runs through it. `EntropyC` imports
   `Entropy` (import graph stays a DAG), not the other way round.
6. **This is not a review.** Evidence tier NUMERICAL, `pending-review:` R15.
